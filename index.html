How this hub works

Every product on this site is archived in DataONE, which stays the permanent, citable record (DOI, version, license). This hub is the front door: it explains what each product is, where and when it applies, how to load it, and how to cite it. Pages are rebuilt automatically from the DataONE metadata, so the two never drift apart.
